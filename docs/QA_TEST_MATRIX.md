Every row inherits its section precondition, data boundary and rule reference. Steps are sequential; submit only clearly marked QA data. Evidence basenames refer to ignored `test-output/ashok-live-qa/<name>.json/.png`.

Status BLOCKED means the row names a specific missing permission, feature, fixture or evidence capability. It is not a pass or proof of product failure. No deleted/archived test cleanup is permitted.
